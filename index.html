Why we exist

Takeaway that's good for you, not just good.

The Goodness Kitchen started with a simple question: why does fast food have to feel heavy? We kept the flavours Sri Lanka loves — kottu, fried rice, nasi goreng, wok noodles — and changed how they're made. Better oil, fresher vegetables, smaller batches, and a lot of love.

The goodness inside

Why it's better for you.

Healthy shouldn't mean boring. Here's what goes into every box, and what stays out.

Rice bran oil

Naturally contains vitamin E and gamma-oryzanol, and has a high smoke point, so it stays stable over a hot wok.

Fresh vegetables

Crunchy, colourful, prepped the same day — fibre and freshness in every bite.

Cooked to order

No reheated trays. Every dish is cooked when you order, so nothing sits around.

Balanced portions

Proper protein, rice or noodles and greens together, so you're full without feeling heavy.
